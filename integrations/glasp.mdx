[Glasp](https://glasp.co) is a web highlighter for saving and organizing what you read online. With this integration you can bring your Raindrop.io highlights, notes, highlight colors and tags into Glasp, and keep them in sync.

## Connecting

<Steps>
	<Step>
		In Glasp, open **Settings → Integrations** and click **Sync Now** on the Raindrop.io card
	</Step>
	<Step>
		Click **Connect Raindrop.io**. You are taken to Raindrop.io to allow Glasp to read your account, then brought back to Glasp
	</Step>
	<Step>
		Click **Sync Now**. New highlights then sync automatically every day
	</Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
	Glasp only reads your Raindrop.io data. Nothing is written back to your Raindrop.io account. Bookmarks without highlights are not imported.
</Note>

In the Glasp app for iPhone and Android, the same import is under **Add → Import → Raindrop.io**.

## Importing a CSV file

You can also move your highlights without connecting your account.

<Steps>
	<Step>
		[Export a CSV file](/export) from Raindrop.io
	</Step>
	<Step>
		In Glasp, open **Settings → Integrations**, click **Sync Now** on the Raindrop.io card, and upload the file under **Or import from a CSV export file**
	</Step>
</Steps>

## FAQ

<AccordionGroup>
	<Accordion title="Why are some of my bookmarks missing in Glasp?">
		Only bookmarks with highlights are imported. Bookmarks without highlights stay in Raindrop.io.
	</Accordion>
	<Accordion title="How often do new highlights sync?">
		Once a day automatically. To sync right away, click **Sync Now** on the Raindrop.io card in Glasp.
	</Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
