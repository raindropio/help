[ChatGPT](https://chatgpt.com/) is an AI assistant by OpenAI. With this integration it can search, read, and organize your bookmarks via [MCP](/integrations/mcp).

<Callout icon="badge-check" color="#FFC107" iconType="regular">
	On the Raindrop.io free plan, the ChatGPT plugin is limited: text and semantic search are not available.
	To get full access, consider upgrading to a Raindrop.io [Pro plan](/premium-features).
</Callout>

## Connecting
Available on all ChatGPT plans, including Free.

<Steps>
	<Step>
		Open [**Plugins**](https://chatgpt.com/plugins), click **Add** in top right corner and select **Add custom MCP server**
	</Step>
	<Step>
		Set name to `Raindrop` and paste the URL to `Connection` field:
		```
		https://api.raindrop.io/rest/v2/ai/mcp
		```
	</Step>
	<Step>
		Check **I understand and want to continue** and click **Create as a plugin**
	</Step>
	<Step>
		Click **Connect Raindrop** and allow access
	</Step>
	<Step>
		In a new chat, type `@` and select **Raindrop**
	</Step>
</Steps>

You only need to connect once. After that Raindrop.io is available in ChatGPT on the web, in the desktop app, and on iOS and Android.
