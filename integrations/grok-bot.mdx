[Grok Bot](https://x.ai/bot) is an AI agent by xAI. With this integration it can search, read, and organize your bookmarks via [MCP](/integrations/mcp).

The bot works on a schedule and signs in to your other apps, so it can also bring links to Raindrop.io on its own. For example, [sync your X bookmarks](#syncing-x-bookmarks) or [collect links from newsletters](#saving-links-from-newsletters).

## Connecting

Send this message to your bot:

```text wrap
Add a custom MCP server called Raindrop.io at https://api.raindrop.io/rest/v2/ai/mcp
```

The bot does the setup on its own. When it asks, authorize Raindrop.io in the browser.

## Ideas

### Syncing X bookmarks

Posts you bookmark on X can be copied to Raindrop.io automatically. Send this message once:

```text wrap
Save all my X bookmarks to Raindrop.io, in a collection called "X bookmarks". Then check every day and add the new ones.
```

### Saving links from newsletters

Good links often get buried in email newsletters. The bot can go through your Gmail on a schedule and pick them out:

```text wrap
Every morning, go through the newsletters that arrived in my Gmail since yesterday. Save the links worth reading to Raindrop.io, in a collection called "Newsletters", and tag each one with the newsletter name.
```

The same works for other apps the bot can sign in to. Change the app, schedule, [collection](/collections), or [tags](/tags) in the message to fit your routine.

If the bot doesn't have access to an app yet, it asks you to sign in first.
