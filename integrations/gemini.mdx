[Gemini](https://gemini.google.com/) is an AI assistant by Google. With this integration it can search, read, and organize your bookmarks via [MCP](/integrations/mcp).

Requires a personal Google Account with **Keep Activity** turned on. Work and school accounts are not supported.

## Connecting

Custom apps can only be added in the Gemini web app on a computer.

<Steps>
	<Step>
		Go to [Gemini connectors](https://gemini.google.com/customize/connectors)
	</Step>
	<Step>
		Click **+ Custom**
	</Step>
	<Step>
		Paste the URL:
		```
		https://api.raindrop.io/rest/v2/ai/mcp
		```
	</Step>
	<Step>
		Click **Next**
	</Step>
	<Step>
		Authorize Raindrop.io in the browser
	</Step>
</Steps>

You only need to connect once. After that Raindrop.io is also available in the Gemini mobile app.

<Tip>Type `@` in the prompt and select **Raindrop.io** to make sure Gemini uses it for that message.</Tip>

Gemini asks for your confirmation before making any changes to your library.

<Note>On Gemini Enterprise a team administrator adds MCP servers for the whole team under **Manage team → Connected apps**. See [Google's guide](https://support.google.com/g/answer/17106276) for details.</Note>
